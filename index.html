Step 1 of 2

Enter your Spotify Developer keys

Because of Spotify's API rules, each device owner needs their own free Spotify Developer app (and a Spotify Premium account). It takes about a minute.

💻 Recommended: do this on a laptop or computer — you'll be copy/pasting a couple of long keys, which is easiest with a real keyboard.
1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and click Create App.
2. Name it anything (e.g. "My BenchLabs Remote").
3. For the Redirect URI, paste exactly:
https://porterh9.github.io/Spotify-Remote/index.html
4. Leave every other box on that form blank — description, website, other redirect URIs, API/SDK checkboxes, all of it. None of it is needed.
5. Save, open Settings, and copy your Client ID and Client Secret below.
